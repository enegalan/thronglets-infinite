# Video: what is still missing

Playthrough: [Black Mirror: Thronglets, full game and ending](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UtreOZO9A3s) (TapGameplay, no narration, 2 h 9 min, 7774 s).

Only what that playthrough shows and this game does not do, or what stays out. What is already in is not repeated: the path laid over the grass, the tray, the console with glyphs, the bubble, the factory ring, the task banner, the late biomes.

## How it was watched

There are no subtitles and the audio was not heard. One 640×360 frame was taken every minute (130, from 0:00 to 129:00). These were opened in full: 0:00, 0:20, 1:00, 3:00, 4:00, 7:00, 8:00, 11:00, 16:00, 17:00, 20:00, 32:00, 35:00, 37:00, 40:00, 46:00, 59:00, 64:00, 68:00, 81:00, 91:00, 92:00, 96:00, 104:00, 106:00, 114:00, 118:00, 124:00, 127:00, and 128:00. The text below is what was read in those frames. This capture is in English. This project's dialogue book stays in Spanish on purpose.

## Opening

- 0:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t000-00.jpg)). TUCKERSOFT logo in rainbow, and over it the recorder's label ("Welcome iTapGameplay"). The label belongs to the capture: it greets the channel, it does not ask for a name.
- 0:20 ([screenshot](video-frames/t000-20.jpg)). Green boot console, not the logo. It reads `imgmount c TUCK96.IMG`, the drive `c:\users\tuck\desktop\tools`, `boot -l c`, `Starting TS-DOZ`, `HIMEM is testing extended memory...`, and a `dir` with `THRONGLETS.COM`, `NOHZDYVE.COM`, and `TSOFT_EDITOR.COM`, dated 08-01-96. A green face sits at the top right. Here the boot is the logo and the title, without that DOS session.
- 1:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t001-00.jpg)). Settings over the title: `Video Captions`, `CRT Overlay`, `Language` set to `English`. Along the bottom, a row of icons (a blue square, a cog, a speaker, a pencil). The CRT filter is not here.
- 3:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t003-00.jpg)). Inside the wood frame, one thronglet and a wood sign: `Oh! It needs a little clean!`. The tutorial of the first minutes is that frame with lines, before the HUD.
- 4:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t004-00.jpg)). The first two are still inside the frame, with no tray, no counter, and no console.
- 7:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t007-00.jpg)). The HUD is up. The counter reads 0. The banner says `GROW THRONGLET NUMBERS`. The tray holds an apple, a ball, a sponge, and the hammer.
- 8:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t008-00.jpg)). The wood counter reads `?`, not a number. The `?` is not here.

## Gestures, mailbox, and unlocks

- 11:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t011-00.jpg)) and 17:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t017-00.jpg)). The hammer drags a white arc and the target (a rock) gets a green box. At 17:00 the banner is `BUILD A BRIDGE` and, over a group, there is a black line of code: `>> data == ♥ makes ♥`.
- 20:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t020-00.jpg)). Parchment card. The top reads `unlocked BUG`. Inside, a picture of a color glitch, `this gift from creator`, `help bridge faster`, and a `&&continue` button.
- 35:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t035-00.jpg)). A red British mailbox on the grass. The console shows, as drawings, a ball, an apple, and a thronglet, and writes that ore is processed to make energy. A blue button with an `i` floats on the right.
- 37:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t037-00.jpg)). The banner is `CRACK ROCKS & COLLECT GEMS`. Next to the counter there is a `New item!`. The mailbox is still on the ground. The hammer still has the white arc over a rock.
- 59:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t059-00.jpg)). The unlock card, titled `New Item!`: `You've unlocked THEATRE!`, the masks, `Entertains Thronglets & applies a speed buff`, and `Continue`. Here an unlock does not open that card.

## Factory, columns, and game over

- 40:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t040-00.jpg)). The banner is `SPEND GEMS; GROW THRONG`. The counter splits in two: gems (86) and population (22).
- 81:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t081-00.jpg)). Population 966, gems `229,929`, banner `UPGRADE STRUCTURES`. Beside a building on the left, a column of round yellow and black pieces rises, with a red plate that says `2`, and it leaves the top of the frame.
- 91:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t091-00.jpg)). Several columns of purple crystals, taller than the buildings, with the console open and the game still going. The cannon is visible beside them.
- 92:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t092-00.jpg)). The banner is `SEND THRONGLETS TO THE VOID`. Gems are around `2,427,210` and the population reads 1. A card says `Game Over`, with a glitched scroll and the red text `OverflowException! Arithmetic operation resulted in an overflow`. The video continues after that. That recoverable game over is not here.

The ring and the pile are not the same thing. The ring drawing has 8 wedges: at 8 or more, the ring stays full. At minute 91 ore is still coming in and the columns pass the height of the buildings, so the pile is not cut off at 8. The gem count does not measure the queue. A factory that empties and fills again adds up fast if each cycle pays like the `+5000` in the capture.

## Fourth wall, captcha, and ending

- 32:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t032-00.jpg)). Behind the frame, the world is the glitch. Inside it floats a square of thronglet faces. The console offers `&& speak` and `return`, and writes `insufficient` and `[interface local.architecture]`. Here the glitch is the sweep when a talk opens. It does not replace the world.
- 96:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t096-00.jpg)). A full-screen report. One column judges the player (`Engaged with the Throng...`, `Killed at least one Thronglet with the Chainsaw`, `Tried to Nuke the void`). The other is titled `NEW DATA DECRYPTED` and lists `VOL 15`, `EFF 16`, `CAR 37`, `INQ 39`, `PRO -3`, `CRE 34`.
- 104:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t104-00.jpg)). A captcha over the void: `SELECT ALL OF THE DEAD THRONGLETS`, nine cells, some with a dead thronglet and blood, and two buttons, `WHAT IS THIS?` and `VERIFY`.
- 114:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t114-00.jpg)). The thronglet is seen up close. A white panel says `THRONG.PACKAGE -> ISOLATING AND TERMINATING SECURITY PERSONNEL WHERE NECESSARY`, with a pink and blue bar. The edge of the shot is broken pink geometry.
- 118:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t118-00.jpg)). A second console, on the left, lists addresses (`throng034.218.19.240`). The choices at that moment are `Can't you teach it English?` and `What's it saying, though?`.
- 124:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t124-00.jpg)). The world is a field of green dots. The console continues: `Neurons run on electricity, after all...`.
- 127:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t127-00.jpg)). Result. A radar, four traits (`Inquisitive`, `Faith faltered at the Last`, `Touched Grass`, `Woody Statue`), `human type: LOVER`, and the buttons `SHARE` and `CONTINUE`. The card is not here. Sharing it stays out.
- 128:00 ([screenshot](video-frames/t128-00.jpg)). Credits in green on black (`// MARKETING`, `// STUDIO LEADERSHIP`). There are no credits.
